# The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower 🗼 — storyboard (master prompt v10.4, boards Z1/Z2)

**Look.** Same look as the Boston video: white bean-head characters with plum outlines, an ink-outlined painted 3D world and a gold accent (#FFB627).
**Cast.**
- Victor Lustig: grey fedora, wine waistcoat, gold watch chain.
- André Poisson: ginger moustache, brown tweed.
- Madame Poisson: teal cloche.
- Three rival dealers, two tower painters, a waiter, a gendarme, a newsboy, Parisians, two clerks, a modern car salesman and his customer.

**Sets.** Seven sets with eleven lighting setups:
- tower: 1925 day, 1889 fair, gold payoff, ending
- street: day and police at dusk
- study at night
- hotel salon: day and night blue
- ministry at night
- car lot at night
- train at golden dusk

**Light script.** Warm and bright for the setup. Deep night blue from the whisper (37 s) through the car-salesman comparison (50 s). A gold sun for the payoff and train. Lavender dusk for the police, then a bright morning for the ending.

**Camera rules.**
- Three hard cuts, all on punch words: S09 "7,000", S15 "trick" and S19 "Vienna".
- Every change of place is a 6–8 frame dissolve.
- A new shot in the same place starts as a camera glide from where the last camera stopped.
- Lustig faces three-quarter or profile, or is seen from behind in over-the-shoulder shots, so he is centred and facing the camera for well under 30% of the runtime.
- Extras are already at work in every set.

The video ends on "selling?" at 61.2 s, and that word loops into frame 1.
