# The Rat Poison Gold Medal 🏅: storyboard (master prompt v10.5)

**Look.** The same kit as the Eiffel Tower video: white bean-head characters with plum outlines, an ink-outlined painted 3D world, and a new accent, track red-orange #FF5A36.
**Cast.**
- Thomas Hicks (white singlet with a red stripe, bib 20) and his coach (bowler, round glasses, ginger moustache), plus a second handler.
- The organizer (top hat, mutton chops) and the official in the car (striped blazer, boater).
- The dust runner, the Cuban mailman (mail cap, cut-off trousers), the runner chased by the dog, and Lorz (bib 31).
- A pharmacist, a coffee customer, farmers, fans, a farm dog and a rat.

**Sets.**
- The stadium, for the start and the finish.
- One long road with five zones: well, dust, orchard, farm and hill.
- A drugstore at night.

**Light arc.**
- Hot bright day for the setup; a lower orange sun on the hill for the poison.
- Deep night blue in the drugstore for the explanation ("That's the trick", 41.5 s).
- The brightest gold for the finish and payoff.

**Camera rules.**
- 2 hard cuts, on "poisoning" and "dead"; 4 dissolves of 8 frames each, only between places.
- Every other change is a camera move that travels to people already there.
- Hicks is never centred and facing the camera for long: three-quarter, profile and over-the-shoulder views.
