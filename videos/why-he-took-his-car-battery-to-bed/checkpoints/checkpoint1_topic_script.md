## Facts and sources

1. **Cold saps the battery while the engine needs more.**
   - AAA: at 32°F a car battery loses about 35% of its strength, and at 0°F about 60%. In the cold, starting can take up to twice the current. Sources: [AAA Hudson Valley](https://www.hudsonvalley.aaa.com/?p=10824), [AAA Oregon](https://info.oregon.aaa.com/winter-battery-preparation/).
   - The script says "at forty below, more than half". That is conservative, because it is already 60% at 0°F.
2. **Why.** Cold slows the battery's chemical reaction, and the engine oil thickens. Sources: [AAA Mountain West](https://mwg.aaa.com/via/car/how-cold-weather-impacts-battery), [AAA Club Alliance](https://cluballiance.aaa.com/membership/seasonal-car-battery-tips), [Mother Earth News](https://www.motherearthnews.com/nature-and-environment/car-starting-tips-zmaz87jfzgoe).
3. **People really did this.**
   - In cold country, people took the battery inside at night. One account from Barrow, Alaska: "people used to literally drain the oil and take the battery out every night" ([forum, Floyd Davidson](https://www.motorsforum.com/ford/how-important-is-it-to-first-warm-up-the-car-winter-62388-3.htm)).
   - More accounts: [Straight Dope (Quebec, Canada)](https://boards.straightdope.com/t/a-battery-warmer/796908) and [EcoModder (Alaska)](https://ecomodder.com/forum/147305-post6.html).
4. **A warm battery stays warm for hours.** In one test, a battery at 65°F took about 12 hours to cool to 0°F in −40°F air ([Mother Earth News](https://www.motherearthnews.com/homesteading-and-livestock/bootstrapping-with-jumper-cables-zmaz83ndzale/)).
5. **About 40 lb.** The average lead-acid car battery weighs about 40 lb, within a 30-50 lb range ([Jerry](https://getjerry.com/questions/how-heavy-is-a-car-battery)).
6. **Dramatized, not claimed as fact:**
   - the logging camp and its people;
   - the steam off the warm battery;
   - the foreman's bunk collapsing at the end.
