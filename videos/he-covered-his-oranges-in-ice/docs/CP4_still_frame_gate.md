## Fixed during the gate (each was a FAIL in an earlier round)

**Size and slicing (1, 12)**
- Every face was low, sitting in the caption band. The v13 rig puts the head centre at 1.30 m, not 1.55 m, so every camera key was re-solved with a projector script. Speaking faces now sit at 35-45% from the top.
- The QA head heights in world.ts were corrected the same way.

**Clear line to the subject (2)**
- **Shot 1:** the glide sliced Earl's hat at the bottom edge.
- **Shot 4:** Duane's thermometer board and the bet crate hid Earl. The thermometer moved to the next post, and the crate now sits on the ground.
- **Shot 8:** the camera sat inside the gate tree's leaves. That glide now runs along the moonlit face of the iced hero tree.
- **Shot 19:** a fence post stood across Earl's face, so the camera moved to Earl's side of the fence.
- **Shot 20:** a post stood across Duane's body. The camera now sees him between two posts.
- **Shot 22:** the crate on the rail hid Duane. He now tosses the crate over the fence to Earl's feet.

**People on the beats (4)**
- **Shot 2 crane:** the frame showed only dark ground.
- **Shot 4 two-shot:** Duane was out of frame.
- **Shot 7:** the farmhands were out of frame.
- All three were re-framed with Earl plus the doers in frame.

**Rows and stacking (6)**
- The farmhands were spread so no head sits behind another.
- In the town shot, Earl was removed so no head stacks behind his, and the four townsfolk were spread across the frame.

**Prop and world (7, 8)**
- The bough fell upward (a wrong rotation axis). It now drops and lies flat on the ground.
- The hose jet came out of thin air. It now leaves the hose tip in his left hand, on the screen side facing the tree.
- The thermometer's red column was hidden by its glass tube.
- The sign was nailed across the gate opening, facing the road. It is now a board the kid holds up, lit and facing the camera.
- The fence was taller than the v13 figures (top rail 1.22 m). It was lowered to 1.1 m posts with the top rail at 0.92 m.
- The scarecrow's legs went through the porch steps. He now stands on the top step.

**Colour and brightness (8, 9)**
- Night frames measured 10-48% near-black. A night grade and a dawn grade were added with lifted black points and more bounce light.
- Near-black is now 0.2-0.7% at night, in the cellar and at sunrise.
- Frame means rose from 27-81 to 84-137.

**Caption band (12)**
- The caption font file was missing, so captions fell back to Arial. Montserrat 900 was installed.
