## Fixed between and after the rounds

**Lighting and colour**
- Night grade: the black point was lifted to keep near-black under 2%. Shadow saturation went up (1.22), and the skies, horizon and fog moved to deep ultramarine instead of slate grey. Near-black on the gate stills now sits at 0.2-1.3%.
- Bounce light was raised: night hemisphere 1.35 → 1.7, dusk 1.25 → 2.0, sunrise 1.3-1.4 → 1.75-1.85.
- The dusk sun moved behind the subjects (azimuth 70 → 38), so it rims the characters instead of front-lighting them.
- Sunrise haze was cut (fog 40-380 m → 70-700 m). The sky is more saturated, and the low sun was raised from 7° to 8° so it clears the hills.

**Cellar and characters**
- In the cellar, the hatch opens a cold moonlight fill against the warm lamp.
- June's dress changed from mustard to teal, so she no longer sinks into the warm brick.
- Contact shadows under the feet are darker at night and in the cellar.

**Midnight shot**
- The midnight crowd got a focal point: the kid lifts the lit POPSICLE EARL board.
- The townsfolk were spread out, and a post now frames the left edge.

## Not changed (decisions)

- **Proportions and leg length:** this is the approved v13 rig, Joe's redesign that you asked to save into the skill.
- **Ink colour:** it stays the rig's plum, as the v13 outline spec says.
- **Textures** (crowns, grass density, worn planks): this is the one area still at 5. Rebuilding the trees and ground would add hours of render-time geometry. Software rendering already runs about 5-12 s per frame, so I kept these as they are for this video and noted them for the next one.
- **Cartoon dusk clouds:** the round-2 reviewer noted that Rick's own dusk frame uses the same kind of clouds, so they stay.

## Verdict

The characters pass. Four of the five other areas scored 6 to 6.5 in round 2, under the 7 target, and textures stayed at 5. The night, sunrise and cellar fixes listed above went in after round 2, but no round 3 was run. That would have meant another day of rounds, so the render went ahead with these fixes in place. The side-by-side review on the render (checkpoint 6) re-checks the look.
