## Side-by-side review

`CP6_side_by_side_1-3.jpg` (private, because they contain board crops) put our frame every 2 s on the top row, with the board frames for the same light below.

The three sheets cover dusk and the night grove, the night and the cellar, and the cellar and sunrise.

- **Characters.** They read as the board's flat white heads with thin black limbs. Faces stay at 35-45% from the top in every speaking shot, and the caption band (62-78%) stays clear of faces.
- **World.** The world is a lit 3D grove with real depth of field and the foreground in focus. The ice coat, sprinklers and smudge-pot fire give it moving light.
- **Night.** With the v2 post grade, the night is closer to the board nights: a blue ultramarine sky, lifted blacks and warm lantern pools. It is still a little darker than Rick's median; see the final table.
- **Gaps against the boards.** Background detail is a little plainer: the lens pass softens edges and the rows repeat. Idle motion is lower between beats. Both are in the final table with reasons.
