# CP1: "They Banned Cars One Day a Week and Got More Cars"

## The one-sentence test (v25, 2.1)
**A city banned every car from driving one day a week, and ended up with about 325,000 more cars and air that was no cleaner.**
- Absurd act: banning every car one day a week.
- Opposite outcome: more cars, not fewer.
- Number: about 325,000 extra cars (one economist's estimate, see Facts).

PASS.
