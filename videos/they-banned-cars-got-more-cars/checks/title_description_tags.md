**Description**

In 1989 Mexico City banned every car one weekday, by plate number. So drivers found the loophole: a second, older car with a different plate. Economists estimate about 325,000 extra cars and no cleaner air. (Memo and Hugo are invented; the ban, the numbers and the 2008 Saturday rule are real. Some researchers argue the second-car effect was smaller than that estimate.)

Was the guy with two cars cheating, or just doing the math?
