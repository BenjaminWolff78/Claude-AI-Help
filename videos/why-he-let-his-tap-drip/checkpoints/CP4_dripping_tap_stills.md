## Fixed after looking at the stills
- **The frost on the kitchen windows** first read as big white crosses over the glass. It is now fine feathers along the edges, lit like the room.
- **The houses** were dark wood in the thaw light. They are now flat painted clapboard: his yellow, the neighbor's grey-blue, then white, green, pink and lilac down the street.
- **The flash-forward** first looked down the side yard, where no spray was in frame. It now looks down the row from his front yard: him sipping in the foreground, the neighbor's house and the next one spraying.
- **The DRIPPY sign** faced away from the camera. It now faces the camera.
- **The neighbor at his taps** faced away from them. He now turns to his taps and strains on the pipe wrench.
- **The cutaways** are shot from the side yard: the clapboard under the window slices away, with the man at his sink through the glass above it. The pipe is thicker and the water bluer.
- **The neighbor in the cutaway and the final shots** was hidden behind the wall. He now stands behind his taps, where the window shows him.
- **Pops at scene changes:** I checked all 8 glide boundaries as frame pairs. Five popped (the old man in his window, the street cast, the neighbor at the fence, the neighbor in his kitchen, the plumber). Each one now carries across, walks in or walks off.
- **Two whips** filled the lens with siding. They now sweep through open yard onto the window.
- **The sampler crashed** with out-of-memory errors. Turning a light on or off changed the light count and recompiled every shader. The lights now only dim.
