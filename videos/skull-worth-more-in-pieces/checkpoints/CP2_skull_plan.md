# CP2: "The Skull That Was Worth More in Pieces": voice, scenes, shots, faces, sound

## Voice (step 2)
- **Voice:** "The Pharaoh 3" on eleven_v4. Take C1: the whole script in one take, with an `[excited]` tag.
- **Processing:** every pause trimmed to 0.10 s (energy-checked, so no word tail is cut). One Rubber Band R3 pass at **T 1.06** (natural pitch, formants preserved).
- **Result:** 65.83 s, last word at 65.52 s. words.json diffed against the script: all 217 words present (only digits vs words differ).

**Decisions:**
- **Renamed the hero Budi to Agus.** The aligner heard "Budi" as "booty".
- **Cut two lines from the take** to stay under 66 s: "The blacksmith sells forty hammers a day." and "Wait, how does a genius fall for a hammer?" The middle escalation (Friday 3,000 / one night 100 / end of month) is untouched.
- **Accepted a slow pace (open item).** This voice reads this script at about 4.25 syllables per second even at the 1.06 tempo cap (target 4.6-4.9).
  - Four full takes all ran 75-76 s of speech before tempo.
  - A test take with a `[quickly]` tag was no faster (16.6 s vs 16.9 s for the same three sentences).
  - The rules forbid tempo above 1.06 and say to cut words instead. Cutting words fixes the length but not the pace, so I'm reporting it here rather than squeezing the voice.

## Expression plan (the eight faces)
- **Defaults:**
  - Agus: calm half-lid (never shock).
  - Joko: grin, mock laughing on the laughs.
  - Ralph: moustache mouth (authority) when buying.
  - Farmers: laugh.
  - Elder: anger.
  - Kids: happy.
  - Sri: calm/smile.
- **Shock (4):** Ralph 859 (fit), Joko 1020 (paid anyway), Ralph 1604 (notices), Ralph 1860 (the slap).

  - 372-455: Joko laughing vs Agus calm.

  - 1181-1264: elder angry vs Agus smug.
  - 1859-1972: Ralph shocked vs Agus deadpan smirk.
