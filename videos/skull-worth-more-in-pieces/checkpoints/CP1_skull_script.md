- **Hero (Agus):** never explains himself. He just smashes.
- **Mockery escalates:** Joko alone, then the whole line, then the whole village (which joins in).
- **Nickname:** "Mr. Hammer", from Joko at the fake-out; it returns at the end.
- **Planted detail:** "One piece, pal" comes back changed, from Agus, at the end.
- **Number in the world:** a painted board on Ralph's table reads "10¢ PER PIECE". At the end it is repainted "WHOLE SKULLS ONLY".
- **Ending:** ironic, and it loops into the hook.
