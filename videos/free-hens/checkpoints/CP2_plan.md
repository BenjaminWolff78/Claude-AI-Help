## Totals and checks built into the plan
- **Shots:** 17, every one 1.5 s or longer. No sub-1 s chains are needed.
- **Hard cuts:** 3 (787, 1034, 1498), none within 1 s of another.
- **Place changes:** A→B at the cuts. B→A at 857 is a motivated crane over the garden wall.
- **Calm stretches (3-5 s):** 7, at 0-3.9, 9.2-12.2, 21.3-26.2, 31.5-34.5, 38.4-41.4, 46.1-49.9 and 49.9-55.0 s. That is at least 5.
- **Shot changes:**
  - planned: about 20 (whips, rises, cranes, the tilt with the bin and the cuts), so about 3.7 per 10 s;
  - Rick's range is 4.0-6.1, and the Library video ran 8.2;
  - I'm keeping it on the calm side of Rick's range, and the previz kit will measure it.
- **Signature:** the Loophole Key is in Odile's hand at "That's the trick" (shot 12). The unlock sting plays at 1034 and is the one stinger.
- **v19 visibility:**
  - truck and café glass is a light tint, never a coloured see-through panel;
  - no camera inside a closed box: the hopper is open-top and is seen from above in daylight;
  - no flare over the readout, the A-board, the scale or the pledge;
  - steam from Odile's coffee is thin wisps, not a card;
  - the bags and food in the hopper are modelled, so the pile reads as bags and food;
  - foreground only in the corners.
- **Held props**, each attached to a hand on every frame:
  - the hen (Odile, Luc), the fork, the tag, the clipboard and the cup;
  - Henri's drumstick and the baker's baguette;
  - the key, the bowl and the caddy;
  - the toy truck, the egg, the pan and the pledge.

  No prop goes through a face. Odile's cup sits at chest height and is lifted to the side of her mouth, never into it.
- **Music** about 15 dB under the voice. It dips under the quoted line and the fake-out, and is cut on "hens".
