## Description
A town handed out free hens, made everyone promise not to eat them, and watched its garbage shrink. Each hen eats about 150 g of kitchen scraps a day, food that would otherwise go in the bin. Would you take two hens if you couldn't eat them? #shorts #chickens #backyardchickens

Real basis: French and Belgian towns (Pincé in 2012, Mouscron, Antwerp, Colmar) gave hens to households to cut food waste. In several Belgian schemes, households signed a pledge not to eat the hens for two years. The characters and gags are dramatised.
