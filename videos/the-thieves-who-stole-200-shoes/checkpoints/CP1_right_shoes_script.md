## FRAME-1 LINE (8.1)
At 0.0 s: inside the dark shop, lit by a torch and the street lamp through the open shutter, the boss thief (big, waist-up, body about 55% of the frame height) is already sweeping a whole shelf of sneakers into a big box with both arms. Shoes tumble mid-air, and the box shakes as they land. On "not one pair" (2.6 s) the camera pushes past him to the display wall: every shoe on it points the same way.
**Why a stranger won't swipe:** the first picture is a robbery already happening, and the first sentence ends on a contradiction (200 sneakers, not one pair). The viewer can see the clue (every shoe points the same way) but can't explain it yet.
