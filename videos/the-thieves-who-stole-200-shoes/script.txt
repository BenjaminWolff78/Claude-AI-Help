Thieves stole 200 brand-new sneakers in one night, and not one pair could ever be worn. At 3 a.m., three guys roll a tricycle up to a little shoe shop. The padlock beats their bolt cutters twice, so the big guy hangs off them with both feet. On the third try it snaps, and the shutter rolls up on a whole wall of sneakers. The boss waves at the camera, "No alarm and no guard, thanks Sleepyhead!" They sweep the wall into three big boxes, stacked taller than the guy pedaling. By sunrise their hideout is full of sneakers, and the boss counts money. At the shop, the owner just stares at his empty wall and sips coffee. Wait, why isn't he upset? His neighbor laughs that he's ruined, while the crew toasts, "Easiest job ever!" That afternoon, the same neighbor sneaks into the hideout to buy a cheap pair. The first box is all right shoes, so is the second, and so is the third. You see, this shop only puts out the right shoe of each pair, and the lefts wait in the back. Ever found one lonely glove in the car? It's useless on its own. Lots of shoe stores really only put one shoe out, for this exact reason. So the neighbor storms out, and the boss crams two right shoes on. At the shop, Sleepyhead holds a single left shoe up to the camera and waves.
