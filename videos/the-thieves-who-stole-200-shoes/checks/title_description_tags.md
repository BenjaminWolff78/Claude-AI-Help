**Description**

Three thieves cut through a shop's padlock at 3 a.m. and cleaned out a whole wall of brand-new sneakers. The catch: the shop only ever put out the right shoe of each pair, so they got away with 200 shoes nobody can wear. The joke wasn't entirely free, though: the owner really did lose those 200 right shoes, which he can't sell without the stolen half either.
