# Samoa skipped a day — storyboard (motionmaxxing method, adapted for a 9:16 Short)

PAGE — Problem: people assume a calendar can't just lose a day. Audience: all ages, curious scrollers.
Goal: watch to the end + rewatch (loop). Emotion: "wait, that's allowed?!" then delight at the twist.
One true thing: on 29 Dec 2011 Samoa (and Tokelau) jumped the International Date Line; Friday 30 Dec never happened locally.

Ideas considered (different kinds):
1. Kinetic-type essay of dates — rejected (deck/slide risk, no world).
2. Map explainer with arrows — rejected as the spine (generic), kept for one beat.
3. **Chosen: one carried object — the torn-off "FRIDAY 30" calendar page — performs the story** (ripped off, sinks
   at the date line, returns as a pay slip, comes back as a repeated July 4 page). Cover-the-logo test: only this story owns a missing page.

World: the South Pacific at one continuous time of day per act (night → dawn → day → old sepia 1892), one light logic
(moon/sun key light upper-left, soft rim on objects), ocean ground with moving swell, grain 0.03, shutter blur 180°/8 sub-frames.
Accent: turquoise #19D3C5 = "the date line / the missing day" only.
Clock: smooth, shutter blur. Type: Nunito (picked via Adobe Fonts), sentence-level captions in the lower band.
No page chrome: no labels, counters, timecodes, scene titles, progress bars. No stat slides.

Beats (buys / what moves / handoff):
| t | role | buys | picture | handoff |
| 0–3.0 | hook | "a day was deleted" | calendar pad on the beach at night; FRIDAY 30 page is ripped off on "deleted" | the torn page flies up out of frame → it's the carrier |
| 3.0–10 | proof | it really happened, on a date | camera pulls back: Samoa (flag) at night, hut lights go out on "bed", moon whips across the sky, sun up; hut calendar THU 29 → SAT 31 | camera tilts up into the sky where the page drifts |
| 10–12.4 | turn | Friday is gone | the FRIDAY page tumbles into the sea and sinks with rings on "never happened" | rings become the dashed date line |
| 12.4–22.3 | proof | why: neighbours were a day ahead | the date line splits the frame: left = Monday (Sydney skyline, office lights, AU/NZ flags), right = Sunday (Samoa beach, hammock) | Samoa island lifts |
| 22.3–25 | hero | they hopped the line | the island hops across the line (screenshot frame: island mid-air over the turquoise seam, splash below) | splash spray carries the page back |
| 25–28.4 | proof | paid anyway | the FRIDAY page lands on a pay packet, stamped PAID, coins pop | stamp hit = pivot pre-roll |
| 28.4–36.3 | twist + loop | 1892 opposite → July 4 twice | sepia-tinted 1892, the pad shows JULY 4, flips… and it's JULY 4 again; small fireworks; ends by cutting back to the night calendar (loop) | last frame = first frame |

Hero moment (built first): the island in mid-hop over the glowing date line, motion-blurred, ocean splash.
