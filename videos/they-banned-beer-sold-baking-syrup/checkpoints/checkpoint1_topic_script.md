## Facts and sources
The people, the town and the lines are invented. Everything below is checked.

1. **Malt syrup was legal and mostly went into home beer.** When beer was banned, breweries made malt syrup, which was legal because it was sold for bakers and confectioners. Most of it went into beer brewed at home. Sources: [BYO, Brewing During Prohibition](https://byo.com/articles/brewing-during-prohibition/); [Mob Museum, ten brewery products](https://themobmuseum.org/blog/ten-alternative-products-made-by-breweries-during-prohibition/)
2. **Hops in the syrup, sold for bread.** A big brewery sold hop-flavoured barley malt syrup, advertised for baking bread and malted milk. Source: [Mob Museum, artifact spotlight](https://themobmuseum.org/blog/artifact-spotlight-alternative-liquor-products-from-prohibition/)
3. **Yeast too.** Breweries also sold brewer's yeast in grocery stores, in 5-lb cakes. Source: Mob Museum (link 1)
4. **The wink.** A 1920s grocery sign shows a grocer winking in front of shelves of malt syrup cans. Source: [Mob Museum Prohibition site](https://prohibition.themobmuseum.org/the-history/the-prohibition-underworld/bootleggers-and-bathtub-gin/)
5. **One can, 45 bottles.** One can of hop-flavoured syrup, water to make 5 gallons, sugar and one cake of yeast make beer. Five gallons is about 45 twelve-ounce bottles. Sources: [Prohibition Pilsner recipe](https://brewery.org/cm3/recs/13_03.html); [Homebrewers Association, The Lost Art of Homebrewing](https://homebrewersassociation.org/?p=26074)
6. **What malt extract is.** It is the sugar from malted barley, boiled down (point 1). Yeast turns sugar into alcohol and carbon dioxide (basic chemistry).
7. **Exploding home brew.** A 1920s newspaper reports a barrel of seized home brew that "continued to explode" in a jail basement. Source: [Utah Digital Newspapers](https://newspapers.lib.utah.edu/details?id=7128009)
8. **Home beer was illegal, the syrup wasn't.** The law allowed some home wine, but nothing allowed home beer; the syrup itself was legal to sell. Source: Mob Museum (link 4)
9. **Used for inspiration only, not said in the video:** a Prohibition agent wrote that, judging by extract sales, there must be an enormous amount of baking done. Source: [BYO, Homebrewing During Prohibition](https://byo.com/articles/homebrewing-during-prohibition/)
