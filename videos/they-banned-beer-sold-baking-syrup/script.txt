The whole station laughs, and soon the whole town calls him the baker.

So where's all the bread?
Then the brewery sells yeast too, enough to raise a loaf the size of the church.
So the agent knocks on doors, and a granny answers with flour on her hands.
She really is baking.
He says sorry, backs down the steps, and her cellar goes pop.
Then the next house pops. Then the whole street.
That syrup is malt extract, the barley sugar, already cooked.
Add water and yeast, and the yeast eats it and burps out alcohol and bubbles.
It's like pancake mix, except it only makes beer.
One can makes 45 bottles, and cap them too soon, the bubbles blow the caps off.
Breweries really did this, and grocers sold it with a wink.
So the agent heads home, opens his cellar, and finds his wife down there, baking.
