## Facts behind it (checked)
- A US Army base in Georgia (Fort Benning) paid hunters $40 per wild pig tail from 2007 (AP via NBC News, 2008).
- During the bounty the pig density went up 23-130% and the young per adult female rose (Ditchkoff, Holtfreter and Williams 2017, Wildlife Society Bulletin 41(3):548-555). The authors' reason: hunters went for big "trophy" animals and easy returns, not the breeding females.
- Dramatised: the shed, the people, the chalk, the poster and the $50 ending are invented.
