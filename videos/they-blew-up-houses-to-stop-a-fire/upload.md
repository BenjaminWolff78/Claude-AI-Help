**Title:** They Blew Up Houses to Stop a Fire 🔥

**Description:**
London was burning street by street, buckets did nothing, and the mayor went back to bed. So the soldiers found a loophole in fire itself: blow up the houses before it gets there. No fuel, no fire. Genius move, or would you have stopped them? 🔥
