## Facts (checked; dramatised details are marked)
- **Where and when:** World of Warcraft, the Zul'Gurub raid, boss Hakkar, 13 Sep to 8 Oct 2005.
- **How the curse worked:**
  - The debuff jumped to nearby players.
  - It was meant to last about 10 s and stay inside the raid.
- **How it escaped:** dismissed pets kept it and brought it to the cities.
- **What happened in the cities:**
  - It could kill top-level characters in seconds.
  - Unkillable non-player characters became carriers.
  - Healers prolonged the spread.
  - Low-level players warned others away.
  - Some players spread it on purpose.
- **How it ended:**
  - Quarantines were bypassed and restarts failed.
  - The world was reset after about a week.
  - A patch made pets immune.
- **The papers:** Balicer, *Epidemiology* (2007); Lofgren & Fefferman, *The Lancet Infectious Diseases* (2007).
- **Dramatised:** Gary, the buddy, Bram and the bread.
- **Not shown or named in the video:** the real game, the boss name and the company name.
