# They Put a Plague in One Room and It Took a Whole City

## Description
A video game put a deadly curse in one locked room, and one pet carried it out into the cities. Was it the worst bug ever made, or the best pandemic drill science ever got?

This is the real "Corrupted Blood" incident from 2005. A raid boss's curse jumped from player to player. It was meant to last a few seconds and stay in the raid, but pets that were dismissed mid-fight kept it. The pets carried it to the capitals:
- Shopkeepers who couldn't die became carriers.
- Healers kept the sick alive long enough to spread it.
- Some players fled, some warned newcomers away, and some spread it on purpose.
- Quarantines were walked around and restarts brought it back.
- After about a week the world was reset and pets were made immune.

Epidemiologists later wrote it up in Epidemiology and The Lancet Infectious Diseases (2007).

The characters, the city and the bread are made up for the story. The game and the people in it are not shown.
