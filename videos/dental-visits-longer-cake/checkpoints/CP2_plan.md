# Checkpoint 2: scene and shot plan for "He Made Dental Visits Longer and Patients Brought Him Cake 🎂"

Voice: take C, one take, pauses trimmed, a single R3 pass at T 1.06. It measures **4.67 syllables per second** (kit formula; Rick 4.6-4.9) over 61.6 s. The video is 1900 frames (63.3 s).

## The world (one connected set, "dentown")
- **The street** runs along x. Both clinics face each other across it.
- **Hal's Dental (north side, warm):**
  - Mint-green clapboard front with a big front window into the waiting room and a glazed door with a bell. A second window looks into the treatment room. A hanging wooden tooth sign and a chalk A-board on the sidewalk.
  - Waiting room: Mae's reception desk (appointment book, egg timer, pen cup, plant), a bench and chairs, a fish tank, a wall TV, magazines, a rubber plant, rugs. The wall of polaroids starts empty and fills.
  - Treatment room: the dental chair (reclining), the round lamp on its arm (48 segments), the tool tray, a 3-minute hourglass, a sink, the locked drawer (Loophole Key), and a corkboard with the long pain-chart paper strip.
- **Vance's Dental (south side, cold):** chrome-and-navy front with a big window. Inside are rows of chrome chairs, a drill on a stand and venetian blinds.
- **The rest of the street:**
  - North side: Sal's barber shop with a 32-segment striped pole, and a bakery with an awning.
  - South side: the LUCKY LANES bowling alley (sign with a pin and ball) and a diner.
  - Street furniture: plane trees (blossoms in spring), lamp posts, benches, a hydrant, a mailbox, a newspaper box, bins, potted plants, parked cars (wheels 24+ segments) and Pratt's red truck cab.
- **The lab** (a separate room, used for one shot): white tile kept below pure white, a table with two tubs labelled 60 s and 90 s, a thermometer that reads 14→15 °C, a stopwatch, beakers, a fluorescent tube and a wall clock.
- **Grade:** darker than the hens video. Targets are brightness 106-132 and under 3.3% pure white (coats are off-white, no white walls).

## Rules this plan meets
- **Hard cuts: 3** (1027, 1424, 1594). Every other change is a glide inside the one connected set. There are no whip pans, because the kit reads whips as roll.
- **Calm stretches of 3 s or more:** shot 4, shots 11-12, shot 16, shot 18, and the end of shot 21.
- **Animation:**
  - Deliberate actions are blended over 10-20 f with overshoot and settle. Gestures take at least 6 f; reactions (the scream, the jump, the mask drop) take 2-4 f and then settle.
  - Every hand-off is reach, grip, carry, release: the prop changes hands on the frame the hand arrives, and the world copy disappears on the same frame.
  - Walkers have stepping feet and nobody slides.
  - The life layer adds breathing and arm drift, so nobody is frozen.
- **Metaphors shown literally:** the two snapshots are real polaroids on the pain strip; the movie is a real TV; the study is a real lab with real tubs.
- **Round things** (lamp, hourglass, cake, wheels, barber pole, tubs, clock) all have 24-48 segments.
