**What the table says, in one line per rule**
- The verb is always mid-action on screen, by a whole body, with a real object at 15-50% of the frame (rows 1, 3, 11, 20, 24).
- A number or rule becomes a physical thing: a desk sign, a button, a wall of photos (rows 13, 14, 16).
- Explanations are acted with real objects, never drawn (rows 3, 9).
- About a third of the character shots are contact poses: sitting, lying, braced in a seat, pressed on glass, seen from behind (rows 15, 17, 18, 21, 23, 24).
- The sharp thing is what the line is about; soft things live only in the corners or far behind (rows 10, 21, 23).
- Something changes every second inside the shot, and a slow move carries the camera to the next subject instead of a cut (rows 2, 20).
