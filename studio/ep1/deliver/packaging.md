## Description
Your coworker says don't take the raise, because it'll push you into a higher bracket. That's wrong: brackets work like buckets, and only the overflow pays the higher rate. We run Maya's real 2026 numbers ($66,500 salary, $2,000 raise) to show it.

But some money rules work like cliffs. For Dan, 60, on the ACA marketplace, $400 over the line can wipe out a credit worth roughly $9,700 a year. We show the one move that can put him back under it.
