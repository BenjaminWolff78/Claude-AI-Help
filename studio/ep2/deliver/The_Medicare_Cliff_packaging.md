## Description
Linda went $500 over a line, and Medicare charged her $1,148 more a year for it.

The rule is called IRMAA. Tax brackets work like buckets: only the overflow gets taxed. IRMAA works like a cliff. Cross the line by one dollar and the whole step applies. And Medicare decides using your tax return from two years earlier.

In this episode:
- Why a 2024 IRA withdrawal raised Linda's 2026 premium
- The five steps, from +$81.20 to +$487 a month for Part B
- Why married couples pay the step twice
- How spreading income across years keeps you in the bucket
- The one way out: Form SSA-44 for life-changing events

Numbers are the 2026 figures from CMS and SSA. The 2027 lines come out in November. Educational, not tax or financial advice.
