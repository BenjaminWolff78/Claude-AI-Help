# Channel branding: Penciled Out

**Name:** Penciled Out
**Handle to claim:** @PenciledOut. I couldn't check availability from here, so try it first. Backups: @PenciledOutMoney, @PenciledOutFinance.
**Tagline:** money decisions, worked out on paper

### Why this name
"Does it pencil out?" is how people ask whether the math on a money decision actually works. That is the whole channel: one real decision (a raise, a Medicare bill, a balance transfer, rent vs. buy), worked out by hand on paper, with sources on screen. The name covers every topic, not one video. "Bucket or Cliff" stays as a recurring series inside the channel.

### Look (same system as the videos)
- Hand-inked outlines with a slight wobble, cream paper with a faint grid, and a riso-style offset color behind the lettering.
- Lettering: Shantell Sans, a hand-drawn typeface.
- Palette: ink #2A2420, paper #F3E7C9, teal #2F7F7A, mustard #E8B23A, coral #E5533D. Each episode adds its own accent (episode 2 uses berry, #B23A6B).
- Every piece was drawn with code in the same ink style as the episodes. No AI image generator, stock art or templates were used.

### Channel description (paste into About)
Every money rule either fills like a bucket or drops like a cliff, and the difference can cost you thousands. Penciled Out takes one real decision at a time (a raise, your Medicare bill, a 0% balance transfer, rent vs. buy) and works the math out by hand, with sources on screen. No hype, no stock footage, just the numbers, drawn out.
Educational, not tax or financial advice.
